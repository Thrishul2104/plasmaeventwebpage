About PLASMA

PLASMA 2K26 is the flagship technical fest of Jawaharlal Nehru New College of Engineering (JNNCE), Shivamogga. Step through the gate, take on technical challenges, pitch your ideas, and compete for cash prizes alongside students from across the country.

Choose Your Challenge

Browse technical challenges across four tracks.

Engineering & Design

Circuits, hardware builds, design and core-branch challenges.

Code & Compute

Hackathons, coding battles, AI and software problem solving.

Research & Presentation

Paper presentations, posters and project showcases.

Enterprise & Expression

Startup pitches, quizzes, creative and non-technical events.
